Concurrency & Synchronization Architecture
POSIX Pthreads
students_sem • Counting Semaphore

Initialized to 0. Represents the number of students currently waiting in chairs. Arriving students who secure a chair execute sem_post(&students_sem). The TA thread waits in sem_wait(&students_sem); if 0, the TA sleeps until signaled.

ta_sem • Rendezvous Semaphore

Initialized to 0. Synchronizes the TA's invitation with the front-of-line student. When the TA is ready to begin a consultation, the TA invokes sem_post(&ta_sem), unblocking the designated student to leave the hallway chair and enter the office.

chair_mutex • Mutual Exclusion

Protects shared simulation state: the occupied chairs array, the circular FIFO queue, arrival timestamps, and telemetry. Ensures that chair allocation and queue modifications are atomic, eliminating race conditions.

Strict FIFO Order & Deadlock Prevention

POSIX semaphores alone do not guarantee arrival-order dispatching. We use a circular queue paired with a condition variable student_called_cv so students are served in exact arrival order. Deadlock is prevented by acquiring chair_mutex only for O(1) state transitions and never holding locks while blocking on semaphores.
